FAQ

Questions, answered.

How soon will I see results? +

Most clients notice performance changes — energy, strength, consistency — within the first three to four weeks. Visible physical change typically follows by week eight.

Do I need gym access? +

No. Programs are built around whatever equipment you have, whether that's a full gym, a home setup, or just bodyweight and bands.

What if I have an injury or limitation? +

Every intake includes a movement and injury history review. Programming is adjusted around what your body can currently handle, and progressed from there.

Can I pause or cancel my plan? +

Starter and Pro plans are billed monthly with no long-term contract. Elite spots are limited and reviewed on a quarterly basis.

How does communication work? +

Depending on your plan, you'll have access to direct messaging, scheduled calls, and structured check-in forms to track progress week over week.
