No. Programs are built around your current level, whether that's day one or year five. The plan adjusts to you, not the other way around.

Not a problem — training plans are built around whatever equipment you actually have access to, gym or home.

How do check-ins actually work?

You submit weekly progress data and photos, and your plan gets reviewed and adjusted within 48 hours — training, nutrition, or both.
